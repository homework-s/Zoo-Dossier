The Meerkat

Learn about the Meerkat!

Information

The meerkat (Suricata suricatta)[1] is a small mammal belonging to the mongoose family. Meerkats weigh around 9 pounds, with a body length of up to 11 inches and a tail up to 9 inches long. Meerkats live underground in burrows up to 16 ft (6 meters).

Habitat

Meerkats are native to South Africa, Angola, Botswana, and Namibia. Their habitats include savannas, shrublands, grasslands, and deserts.

Diet

Meerkats are omnivores, meaning they eat both plants and animals. Meerkats have a high metabolism and need to eat frequently to maintain their energy levels.

  • Insects
  • Small mammals
  • Birds
  • Eggs
  • Fruits
  • Roots

Predators

Meerkats have many predators, including raptors, jackals, and snakes

  • Hyenas

  • A Jackal

Conservation

Meerkats are listed as "least concern" on the IUCN Red List[2], but they are threatened by habitat loss and climate change. Conservation efforts are focused on protecting their habitats and educating the public about the importance of meerkats in the ecosystem.

Behavior

Meerkats are social animals that live in groups called mobs or family. A Meerkat family can have up to 40 members, and every member has a unique role: gathering food, caring for babies, and keeping watch for predators..
