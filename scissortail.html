The Scissortail Rasbora

Learn about the Scissortail Rasbora!

Information

The scissortail rasbora (Rasbora trilineata)[1] is a small fish belonging to the Cyprinidae family. They reach around 3.5 in (8 cm) in length and weigh roughly 2–5g.

Habitat

Scissortail rasboras are native to Southeast Asia, particularly the Mekong River basin across Cambodia, Thailand, Malaysia, and Indonesia.

Diet

Scissortail rasboras are omnivores, feeding on small organisms and organic matter:

  • Daphnia
  • Worms
  • Small Crustaceans
  • Foliage & Plant Matter

Conservation

Scissortail rasboras are listed as "Least Concern" on the IUCN Red List[2]. While not currently threatened with extinction, wild populations are monitored to ensure trade remains sustainable.

Behavior

The Scissortail Rasbora is found in slow-moving streams and rivers with dense vegetation. They are social, peaceful, schooling fish that move in large groups and are known for their active swimming behavior.
