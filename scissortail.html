The Scissortail Rasbora

Learn about the Scissortail Rasbora!

Information

The scissortail rasbora (Betta splendens)[1] is a small fish belonging to the Cyprinidae family. Around 3.5in (8cm) and weighs 36-56g

Habitat

Scissortail rasboras are native to Cambodia, mainly Cambodia's Southern Mekong River basin, including Thailand, Malaysia, and Indonesia

Diet

The scissortail rasboras are omnivores, meaning they eat both plants and animals.

  • Daphnia
  • Worms
  • Small Crustaceans
  • Foliage

Predators

Scissortail rasboras have multiple predators, they are prey to larger fish.

  • The Catfish

  • The Snakeheads

Conservation

Scissortail rasboras are listed as "least concern" on the IUCN Red List[2] They are not necessarily threatened with extinction, but may become so unless trade is closely controlled.

Behavior

The Scissortail Rasbora is found in slow-moving streams and rivers with dense vegetation. They are social, passive and schooling fishes that live in large numbers and are known for their active swimming behavior.
