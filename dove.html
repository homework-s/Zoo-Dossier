The White-Throated Ground Dove

Learn about the White-Throated Ground Dove!

Information

The white-throated ground dove (Gallicolumba xanthonura)[1] they are a small bird belonging to the dove family. Around 26cm (10 in) in length, and 58-140g in weight

Habitat

White-throated ground doves are Native to Guam but has since been scattered around the Northern Mariana Islands

Diet

The white-throated ground doves are Frugivores, meaning they only eat fruits, leaves, flowers, and seeds.

  • Fruits
  • Leaves
  • Seeds
  • Flowers

Predators

White-throated ground doves have one main predator

  • The brown snake

Conservation

White-throated ground doves are listed as "near threatened" on the IUCN Red List[2], they are threatened by an invasive species. Conservation efforts are focused on protecting their habitats and educating the public about the importance of white-throated ground doves in the ecosystem.

Behavior

White-throated ground doves are independent birds, rarely seen in pairs outside of mating season and tend to be shy and kept at a distance. As well as being territorial, they are also known for their distinctive calls and courtship displays.
