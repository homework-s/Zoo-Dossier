The White-Throated Ground Dove

Learn about the White-Throated Ground Dove!

Information

The white-throated ground dove (Gallicolumba xanthonura)[1] is a small bird belonging to the dove family, around 26cm (10 in) in length and 58-140g in weight.

Habitat

White-throated ground doves are native to Guam but have since scattered around the Northern Mariana Islands.

Diet

The white-throated ground doves are frugivores, meaning their diet mainly consists of:

  • Fruits
  • Leaves
  • Seeds
  • Flowers

Predators

White-throated ground doves have one main predator:

  • The Brown Tree Snake

Conservation

White-throated ground doves are listed as "near threatened" on the IUCN Red List[2] and are threatened by invasive species. Conservation efforts focus on protecting their habitats and educating the public about their importance in the ecosystem.

Behavior

White-throated ground doves are independent birds, rarely seen in pairs outside of mating season, and tend to keep their distance. Territorial in nature, they are known for their distinctive calls and courtship displays.
